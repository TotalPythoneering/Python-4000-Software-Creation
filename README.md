## Summary

Any little bit of knowledge can indeed be a very dangerous thing – and
never more so whenever we assume that research and development about
writing code. Indeed, the truth is that most projects are about
demonstrating our solution to a set of forever-changing, real-world
requirements. So in “Gig Success” we will unmask the reality behind
every project – software or not – that is asking us to provide any
modicum of sustainable value so as to better support just about every
ongoing mission.

## Lessons

100: Welcome to 'Gig Success'

Welcome to the 'gig economy.' Here is the what we'll be covering in this
training. Concepts that I believe that we'll find no where else?

---

200: Your Missions

Managing expectations is the very real mission behind every creation
process. Here is a starting set of concepts worthy of a consideration.

---

300: D.I.Y Mission

Whenever there is a need there is a mission statement... and we've often
many of our own needs as well.

---

400: Real-World Gig Descriptions

In the English, actors and actions readily translate down to nouns and
verbs. Let's take a moment to appreciate any 'who', 'what', 'where',
'how', 'why' and 'when' need(s) behind any one of our own missions.

---

500: Requirement Decompositions

Lets take a moment do see if we can detect any 'hidden requirements'
within an extremely common mission-statement - a need that everyone
often has?

---

600: Requirement Expansion

My common Kata, all we often need do is to simply apply our formula to
detect, as well as to honeslty resolve, any deliberate and / or innocent
ambiguities. -Parity matters!

---

700: Why Enumeration Matters

Pride of ownership is just one of the very many reasons why we often
need decompose - as well as to seldom change - any legacy requirement.

---

800: Actors & The Enumerated Use Case

By the time we've defined any final set of all projects / sprint
meta-documents one might reasonably hope to have a unique identifier for
any actionable items and / or deliverables therein?

---

900: Every Project's 'Geek Speak'

The larger any patron's organization the more we'll need to be willing -
or able 🫣 - to fully embrace any project 'domain speak.' - Indeed, the
ability to be less impressed with ourselves and far more impressed with
our clients is usually a 'professional thing'? 😏

---

1000: Completeness Flows & Checks

Low-level walk-thrus using flow charts and / or activity diagrams are
often the only way to insure what we're doing is what everyone expects
us to have done.

---

1100: Requirements & Approvals

Requirements are often the only artifacts to survive our documentation
efforts, so let's take a moment to appreciate how our requirements are
making it over to our code.

---

1200: A.I Code & Updates

Let's take a moment to better appreciate why software development has
today become more about project 'engineering', rather than 'encoding?'

---

1300: Testing Optionals?

Why we'd hate to test a solution like ours - and how we might learn how
to enjoy the challenge.

---

1400: Process Review

Let's lock what we've discovered into our longer-term memories by
reviewing a very probably process-centric Use Case.
